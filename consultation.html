Understanding ED

ED is not one thing

Erectile dysfunction has multiple root causes. The right treatment depends entirely on identifying which type — or which combination of types — applies to you.

🧠

Psychogenic ED

Driven by performance anxiety, stress, depression, or relationship tension. Often affects younger men and is situational — erections may still occur in some contexts but not others.

❤️

Vasculogenic ED

Caused by reduced arterial blood flow, typically linked to cardiovascular risk factors — diabetes, high blood pressure, smoking, or elevated cholesterol. The most common physical cause over 40.

⚗️

Hormonal ED

Related to low testosterone or other endocrine imbalances. Often accompanied by fatigue, reduced libido, and mood changes. Highly treatable once identified with blood testing.

🧬

Neurogenic ED

Caused by nerve pathway disruption — from diabetes-related neuropathy, multiple sclerosis, spinal injury, or following pelvic surgery (prostate, bladder, bowel).

🔄

Mixed ED

The most common presentation. Multiple contributing factors interact — a physical issue can create anxiety, which compounds the original problem. A layered approach is needed.

💊

Medication-Induced ED

Certain common prescriptions — including antidepressants, beta-blockers, and antihypertensives — can impair erectile function as a known side effect. Often overlooked.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is my assessment completely private? +
Yes. The entire questionnaire runs in your browser. No data is transmitted to any server, and no information is stored. We do not collect your name, email, or any identifiable detail for the free assessment.
What is the IIEF and why is it used? +
The International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF) is the gold-standard validated questionnaire used in urology and clinical research worldwide. Our assessment incorporates the IIEF-5 (SHIM) for severity scoring and extends it with additional domains to categorise ED type — not just measure it.
How accurate is this assessment? +
The IIEF-5 has demonstrated sensitivity of 98% and specificity of 88% for detecting ED in clinical validation studies. The ED type categorisation uses additional validated screening questions. However, this is a screening tool — formal diagnosis and treatment require a clinician consultation.
Can I get a prescription from the questionnaire alone? +
No. The free assessment provides information and a profile only. A prescription for ED medication requires a consultation with a registered clinician, available through our Tier 2 and Tier 3 plans. This ensures safe and appropriate prescribing.
What if I haven't been sexually active recently? +
The questionnaire includes options for men not currently sexually active. Questions about morning erections, self-stimulation, and historical patterns will still generate a meaningful profile. Select "no activity" options where applicable.
How soon can I book a consultation after my assessment? +
Immediately. Once you've completed the free assessment you'll be shown booking options. Typical availability is within 48–72 hours, with some same-day appointments available.
Is ED really common in men under 40? +
More common than most men realise. Research suggests up to 26% of new ED cases present in men under 40, and the Massachusetts Male Aging Study found some degree of erectile difficulty in 52% of men aged 40–70. ED in younger men is often primarily psychogenic — which makes it highly responsive to the right treatment.
